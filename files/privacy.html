PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: August 2026

This website serves as the official portfolio, customizer, and order inquiry platform for AS Print Gallery. We do not process direct credit card payments on this website, nor do we sell or monetize personal data.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us or send an inquiry via WhatsApp, telephone, email, or our contact form, we receive the information you choose to provide — including:

  • Your name, business/brand name, and contact phone number.
  • Print artwork, brand logos, dimensions, color preferences, and specifications.
  • Delivery shipping address and pin code for parcel dispatch.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To design complimentary artwork mockups and provide accurate quotations.
  • To print, quality-inspect, and package your custom printing order.
  • To arrange secure courier dispatch and share live tracking updates with you.
  • To maintain proof records for seamless reorders in the future.

3. Confidentiality of Client Artwork & Logos

All brand logos, trademark assets, and custom design files shared with AS Print Gallery remain 100% your intellectual property. We do not reuse, resell, or distribute client designs to third parties.

4. Information Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell or rent client data. Shipping information is shared only with licensed delivery partners (e.g. Bluedart, Delhivery, DTDC) solely for order fulfillment.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You can request copies of your order history, artwork files, or request data removal at any time by messaging us on WhatsApp at +91 99116 78386.
