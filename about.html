AS PRINT GALLERY is an established industrial printing, labelling, and custom packaging manufacturing enterprise located in Loni, Ghaziabad (Uttar Pradesh). Guided by our company tagline "A Complete Designing & Printing Solutions", we manufacture and supply direct-to-brand packaging collateral that powers retail counters, garment manufacturers, e-commerce stores, and food businesses across India.

What We Manufacture

Our in-house press, plotting, and die-cutting machinery specializes in 14 core manufacturing lines:

  • Garment Brand Labels: Printed Care Labels, High-Density Woven Neck Labels, and Heat Transfer Apparel Stickers.
  • Tags & Inserts: Custom Punch-Hole Hang Tags, Price Cards, and Guarantee Sleeves.
  • Self-Adhesive Stickers: Chromocote Gumming Stickers, Waterproof Vinyl Stickers, Barcode/QR Inventory Rolls, and Bottle Labels.
  • Retail & Courier Packaging: Custom Garments Packaging Boxes, Kraft Paper Bags, Branded Envelopes, and Corrugated Master Cartons.
  • Food Packaging: Custom Corrugated Pizza Delivery Boxes and Gold-Foil Sweet (Mithai) Boxes.
