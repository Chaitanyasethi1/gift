🔍 1. What Are Functional Cookies and LocalStorage?

Cookies and HTML5 LocalStorage are tiny text records placed directly in your browser. Unlike third-party advertising trackers, AS PRINT GALLERY only utilizes strictly essential client-side functional keys to keep your session smooth and lightning-fast.

🛒 2. Keys Stored on Your Device

as_cart Stores your selected packaging box models, custom sizes, quantities, and pricing so you can browse freely without cart resets.
as_wishlist Saves your bookmarked cartons, stickers, or woven label specifications for quick re-ordering.
as_latest_order Caches your active order receipt for one-click status tracking and invoice downloads.

🚫 3. Zero Third-Party Advertising Trackers

We believe in clean, privacy-respecting e-commerce. We do NOT run intrusive cross-site behavioral tracking cookies, ad retargeting pixels, or sell analytics telemetry to data brokers.

🧹 4. Clearing Stored Data
