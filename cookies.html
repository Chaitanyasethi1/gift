COOKIE POLICY

Last updated: August 2026

This site keeps cookies to a strict minimum since it is an informational portfolio and interactive customizer site, not an invasive tracking network.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies and local storage are small data files stored on your device to remember basic site preferences (such as your cookie preferences, theme selections, or custom stamp generator states) between visits.

2. What Cookies & Storage We Use

  • Essential & Functional Storage: Required for the website customizer tools, price calculator, mobile menu states, and cookie preference memory (as_print_cookie_consent).
  • Anonymous Analytics (Optional): If enabled, these help us understand aggregate page visits to improve website responsiveness. No personal, financial, or confidential information is collected.

3. Third-Party Integrations

External links to WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube will direct you to those respective third-party platforms, which operate under their own independent privacy and cookie policies.

4. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can clear or block cookies at any time via your browser settings. Clearing essential storage will simply reset interactive tool states to their initial defaults.
