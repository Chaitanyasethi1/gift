🛡️ 3. Packaging & Moisture Protection Standards

All corrugated boxes are bundled in counts of 25 or 50 pieces, strapped using heavy-duty polypropylene banding, edge-protected with corner guards, and wrapped in 50-micron waterproof LDPE shrink film. This ensures that consignments remain unaffected by rain, humidity, or rough transport handling.

Live tracking links are automatically dispatched to your registered mobile and email. You can also monitor real-time shipment status at our Live Dispatch Tracking Portal.

In the rare event of transit damage or broken straps during carrier transit, document the outer cartons with 2-3 clear photographs and contact our logistics coordinator on WhatsApp (+91 9911678386) within 24 hours. We dispatch immediate replacements directly from the factory at zero cost.
